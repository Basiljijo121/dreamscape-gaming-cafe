## 💬 Join the Planning!

We want YOUR ideas to shape Dreamscape! Here's how to contribute:

### 📋 Discussions
Head to the **[Discussions tab](../../discussions)** to:
- Suggest equipment picks
- Vote on cafe design themes
- Recommend games and services
- Share food/drink ideas

### 🐛 Issues / Ideas
Open an **[Issue](../../issues/new)** tagged with:
- `idea` — New concept or feature suggestion
- `equipment` — PC, console, peripheral suggestions
- `design` — Layout, interior, theme ideas
- `food` — Menu items and drink suggestions
- `games` — Game titles and tournament ideas
